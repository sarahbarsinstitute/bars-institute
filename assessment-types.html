BARS Institute · New Zealand

Independent biological evidence for vineyard valuation, lending, and transaction decisions

BARS translates vineyard disease condition into quantified evidence for asset value, credit risk, and acquisition due diligence — expressed in the language of financial decisions, not viticulture reports.

Vineyard biological condition is often assessed visually. In white varieties such as Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, and Pinot Gris, leafroll virus can be present without visible symptoms. A block may appear healthy while carrying material disease burden.

That risk does not disappear at settlement. It transfers to the buyer, lender, and adviser with the asset.

BARS provides PCR-confirmed biological evidence, translated into financial impairment figures for professional reliance in security reviews, valuation adjustments, covenant monitoring, and transaction due diligence. BARS does not replace a registered valuation, accounting opinion, or credit decision — it provides the biological evidence those decisions may require.

What every BARS assessment produces
Block-level virus prevalence
PCR-confirmed — not estimated from visual inspection
Financial impairment
Expected and stress-case NPV in $/ha and % of clean asset value
Replant timing by block
When each block is projected to reach its replant threshold
Independent documentation
Structured for valuation reports, credit memos, and due diligence packages
Assessment types
BARS Report
1–2 visits
The entry-point assessment for transactions, security reviews, and financing decisions — quantified biological risk in the language of credit and valuation.
Lenders · Valuers · Buyers · Transaction advisers

The standard BARS engagement. Quantifies GLRaV-3 virus prevalence and trunk disease burden using composite PCR pool testing, then converts those findings into financial impairment figures suitable for credit and valuation workflows.

Where positive detection is confirmed, full impairment modelling — expected and stress-case NPV, replant trigger year, and intervention value — is produced from the same visit data. No return visit required for the financial outputs.

A second dormancy visit may be added where trunk disease assessment at pruning is required, or where seasonal timing makes a single visit impractical.

Decision-grade outputs
  • Virus prevalence score — GLRaV-3, 0–100
  • Trunk disease incidence score — 0–100
  • NPV impairment P50 and P95 — $/ha and % of clean asset value
  • Replant trigger year per block
  • Intervention value — financial difference between management scenarios
  • Standard sensitivity tables for professional disclosure
  • Independent report structured for valuation and lending files
Enhanced Due Diligence
2 visits
For new owners who need to understand exactly what they have acquired — vine-level confirmation, spatial extent, and pathogen-class precision.
Post-acquisition · Legal and insurance matters · Dispute resolution

Builds on an existing BARS Report to provide vine-level PCR confirmation on all positive bays. Where infection is detected, a second visit maps the spatial extent of clustering — distinguishing isolated results from the edge of a larger infection zone.

Pathogen-class confirmation for trunk disease narrows the uncertainty interval and supports more precise replant and management decisions. The result is the highest-precision biological condition record available for a vineyard block.

Suitable for post-acquisition management planning, legal and insurance matters, and dispute resolution where biological condition is in question.

Decision-grade outputs
  • Vine-level virus prevalence — confirmed by PCR on all positive bays
  • Spatial infection map showing clustering and extent
  • Trunk disease burden with pathogen-class confirmation
  • Vine-level co-occurrence of virus and trunk disease
  • Revised NPV impairment P50 and P95 at vine-level precision
  • Replant trigger year — narrowed uncertainty interval
  • Full sensitivity tables
  • Independent technical appendix for valuation, legal, or insurance use
Prerequisite: Requires an existing BARS Report for the assessed blocks.
Annual Monitor
1 visit
For lenders and owners tracking whether biological risk is moving as projected — and catching divergence before it becomes a credit or management problem.
Covenant monitoring · Portfolio surveillance · Annual credit review support

Updates an existing BARS Report and tests whether disease progression is tracking as modelled. Designed for lenders monitoring biological condition as a covenant condition and for advisers conducting annual credit reviews on vineyard-secured lending.

Where observed spread exceeds the modelled stress case, a management divergence flag is issued and impairment figures are revised for lender disclosure. Where spread tracks within the projected range, that confirmation is also reported — positive evidence that the assumptions in the original assessment remain defensible.

Decision-grade outputs
  • Updated virus and trunk disease scores
  • Year-on-year trajectory comparison against modelled projection
  • Management divergence flag where spread exceeds stress case
  • Revised impairment figures where divergence is confirmed
  • Spatial overlay — year-on-year infection distribution change
Prerequisite: Requires an existing BARS Report as a baseline.

Why it matters
Disease affects yield, replant timing, and asset longevity. Those effects flow directly into valuation, lending security, and transaction risk. BARS turns biological condition into evidence that can be used in those decisions.
BARS outputs are independent decision-support evidence. They do not constitute a registered valuation, accounting opinion, or credit approval recommendation. The application of BARS outputs to valuation adjustments, impairment testing, and lending decisions is a matter of professional judgment for the engaging valuer, lender, or adviser.
