Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Applies to bars.institute, Decision OS, and Vineyard Risk

1. Who we are

BARS Institute Limited ("BARS Institute", "we", "us") is registered in New Zealand and operates two services under this domain: Decision OS, a probabilistic business decision-comparison tool, and Vineyard Risk, a biological asset risk assessment service for vineyard operators, lenders, valuers, and transaction advisers.

We are the "agency" under the Privacy Act 2020 responsible for the personal information described in this policy. We are based in Wellington, New Zealand. You can contact us at sarah@bars.institute, and we'll provide a postal address on request.

2. Scope

This policy covers information collected through bars.institute, the Decision OS application (including its beta), and the delivery of Vineyard Risk assessments. It does not cover third-party sites we link to.

Much of the information we handle — vineyard records, disease results, business financials — is about properties and businesses rather than people. We treat all client information as confidential under our Terms. This policy is about the part that is personal information: information about an identifiable individual.

3. Information we collect

  • Contact details — name, email, and anything else you include when you get in touch, join the waitlist, request an assessment, or book a decision analysis.
  • Access details (Decision OS beta) — the email address we send your invitation to, and the access code we issue to you. The beta has no user accounts or passwords.
  • Decision data (Decision OS) — the business decision, options, and financial assumptions you provide, whether sent to us directly or entered into the application. Information entered into the application is sent to our servers to run the simulation and is not stored: it is held in memory only while the calculation runs, and is not written to a database or kept afterwards. Information you send to us directly, for example by email, is kept as described in Section 11.
  • Vineyard and biological data (Vineyard Risk) — block-level vineyard records, financial data relevant to valuation or lending, ownership or transaction details, and diagnostic results from samples submitted for laboratory testing.
  • Payment data — we do not currently take payments online. When we do, payments will be handled directly by Stripe, and we will not receive or store full card details ourselves.
  • Technical data — your IP address and basic details of each request, which are recorded in our hosting providers' server logs and used briefly by the application to limit misuse.
  • Usage and analytics data — see Section 8.

Providing information is voluntary, but we can't run an analysis or assessment without the information it depends on, and we can't respond to an enquiry without a way to contact you. If you choose not to provide certain information, we may be unable to provide some or all of our services to you.

4. Information we receive about other people

Our clients sometimes give us personal information about other people — for example employees, contractors, co-owners, or job candidates named in a decision, or people connected to a vineyard property or transaction. When that happens, we've collected information about you from someone other than you, and this policy tells you what we do with it.

  • Who has it: BARS Institute Limited, Wellington, New Zealand — contact details in Section 1.
  • Why: only to deliver the analysis or assessment our client engaged us for.
  • Who else sees it: our client, the service providers in Section 9, and anyone our client shares the final report with.
  • Legal basis: collection isn't required by any particular law; our client chose to engage us.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see or correct it — see Section 16.

Our Terms require clients to tell people when they pass their information to us, and to point them to this policy. We ask clients to give us only what an analysis actually needs, and we prefer roles, ranges, or de-identified figures over names wherever that works.

6. Use of data to improve our methods

We don't use client data to develop or calibrate our models.

7. Automated decision-making

Decision OS runs probabilistic simulations. An AI-assisted structuring feature, which uses AI to help translate a described decision into a model, is planned but is not part of the current beta. In either case Decision OS does not itself decide anything about a person. It is not intended to make solely automated decisions about an individual's employment, credit, insurance, housing, or other significant circumstances, even where a decision template (such as a hiring comparison) relates to those topics. Any such decision remains a human decision made by you.

We do not use Decision OS to make automated decisions about access to our services, pricing, or credit for individuals.

8. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to help us understand how the site is used. Google Analytics 4 does not retain full IP addresses. We do not use Analytics for advertising, remarketing, or cross-site tracking. You can opt out using your browser settings or Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Application. The Decision OS application sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in for a limited period (currently 10 days) after you enter your access code. It isn't used for tracking, and the application does not otherwise use browser storage. The application itself does not use Google Analytics.

9. Who we share it with

We use a limited number of service providers to run the services. They process information on our behalf and not for their own purposes, except where noted.

  • Hosting — Render hosts the Decision OS application and its calculation engine, so information you enter passes through Render's servers while a simulation runs. GitHub Pages hosts this website. Both keep standard server logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Email — Google (Gmail), for enquiries, waitlist requests, and anything you send to us directly.
  • Payment processing — not currently in use. When online payment is introduced it will be handled by Stripe, which also uses some payment information for its own purposes, such as fraud prevention, under its own privacy policy.
  • Data storage (Vineyard Risk) — Supabase. The Decision OS beta does not store the information you enter, so it has no data store.
  • AI-assisted structuring (Decision OS) — Not part of the current beta. Once it is enabled, information you enter to describe a decision will be processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, to help structure it into a model. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this information is not used to train its models. The calculation itself is always performed by our own deterministic engine, not by the AI provider.
  • Diagnostic laboratories (Vineyard Risk) — where laboratory testing is required, samples may be referred to an appropriately accredited laboratory. The relevant laboratory, and the scope of its accreditation, depends on the specific test; accreditation does not necessarily cover every disease, assay, or method. We receive the resulting diagnostic data; the laboratory remains responsible for the physical sample and underlying test record.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics.

We do not share client decision data, vineyard data, or diagnostic results with any other party without your consent, except where required by law.

10. International data transfers

Some of the providers above store or process information outside New Zealand, including in Australia and the United States, depending on the provider. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the information is protected in a way consistent with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

11. Data retention

We keep information only as long as we need it. During the beta, Decision OS does not store the information you enter: inputs, models, and results are held in memory only while a simulation runs, and a report exists only in your browser and in any copy you save or print. If saved analyses are introduced later, we will update this policy first. We keep engagement records, audit logs, backups, and payment records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.

12. Deletion

You can ask us to delete personal information we hold about you. We'll action this where we can, but deletion may not be immediate or complete where information is required by law, needed to establish or defend a legal claim, held in secure backups pending their normal cycle, required for accounting or tax records, or necessary to prevent fraud. Deleting our copy doesn't affect any copy of a report you've already received or shared with a third party.

13. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we hold, including relying on providers (Render, GitHub, Supabase, Google) that maintain their own security standards. Access to the Decision OS beta is by individual access code, and its calculation engine is not reachable from the public internet. We restrict access to personal information to staff and contractors who need it to perform their role, and we require them to keep it confidential. No online system is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute protection against unauthorised access.

14. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a privacy breach likely to cause serious harm, we will assess it and notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as required by law.

15. Marketing

We do not send marketing communications unless you've asked for or agreed to them. You can unsubscribe from anything you do receive at any time.

16. Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct anything inaccurate. Contact us at sarah@bars.institute and we'll respond within 20 working days. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity before responding to a request, to ensure we do not disclose personal information to someone who is not entitled to it.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your information or a request, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve it with you first.

If another privacy law applies to your information — for example because you're based in the EEA or UK — additional rights may apply, and we'll assess your request against whatever law applies to it.

17. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business and professional use and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the services change, particularly as the Decision OS application moves out of beta. We'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell people with access to the application by email about material changes. We encourage you to check this page periodically for updates.

19. Contact

Questions about this policy or a request relating to your information: sarah@bars.institute.
