PCR-confirmed GLRaV-3 infection prevalence expressed as a current-condition score from 0–100. Derived from data processed under ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory workflows. No projections, no modelled assumptions — observed biological condition only.

Methodology & Standards

Designed to survive professional scrutiny

BARS assessments are governed by a published Assessment Methodology Statement (MET-BARS-001). Every parameter is explicitly disclosed — calibration status, source basis, and sensitivity ranges.

All diagnostic tissue samples are sent directly to independent, third-party IANZ-accredited laboratories for definitive PCR analysis. Rigid chain-of-custody documentation accompanies every sample from collection through to laboratory receipt.

The core BARS protocol was architected by an established specialist with years of hands-on experience managing plant virus detection inside accredited laboratory systems.
