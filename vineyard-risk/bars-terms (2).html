2. The services

Decision OS compares two business options under uncertainty and reports the probability of each outcome, the assumption the result is most sensitive to, and the point at which the comparison flips. Decision OS is currently available as a fixed‑fee decision analysis prepared by us and, where offered, through a beta application. Beta features may be incomplete, outputs may change as the underlying methodology is refined, and access may be modified, suspended, or withdrawn at any time. Access to the beta is by an individual access code issued by us. The beta application does not store the information you enter or the reports it produces: a report exists only in your browser, so save or print any report you want to keep.

Vineyard Risk quantifies the financial exposure of vineyard biological risk — disease prevalence, spread trajectory, and trunk disease burden — using laboratory diagnostics and probabilistic financial modelling, delivered as a written assessment.
