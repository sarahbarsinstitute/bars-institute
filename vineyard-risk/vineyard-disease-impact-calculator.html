Vineyard Disease Impact Calculator

The BARS Institute calculator is designed to help vineyard owners and managers explore how GLRaV-3, trunk disease and missing vine positions can translate into potential economic exposure.

We are currently refining the model and validating its assumptions before making the calculator available for general use.

We have temporarily closed access while we complete this validation. This means the calculator should not currently be used to make vineyard management, investment or valuation decisions.

What the tool is designed to explore

Connecting biological disease burden with economic exposure

01

Disease burden

Explore how assumptions about GLRaV-3 prevalence, trunk disease incidence and missing vine positions can affect the projected biological condition of a vineyard block.

02

Future cash flow

Translate disease-related yield effects, grape prices, operating costs and replanting assumptions into potential future economic impacts.

03

Decision support

The broader BARS framework is designed to help identify where biological risk may be creating the greatest financial exposure and where further investigation may be worthwhile.

Why we are refining it

A useful calculator needs more than a convincing number.

A disease-impact model is only useful if its biological assumptions, financial assumptions and uncertainty are sufficiently robust for the decision being made.

The public calculator was intentionally simplified. We are now reviewing the model and its assumptions before putting it back in front of users.

The full BARS Assessment goes further by using block-specific evidence, documented assumptions and uncertainty analysis rather than relying on generic assumptions alone.

Full BARS Assessment
Block-specific evidence Disease prevalence and condition are based on evidence from the actual vineyard block.
Biological modelling Disease progression is projected over the remaining productive life of the block.
Financial modelling Disease effects are translated into potential impacts on future vineyard cash flows and capital requirements.
Uncertainty analysis Key biological and financial assumptions are varied to produce a bounded range rather than relying solely on one central estimate.
Decision intelligence Results are intended to help identify where further investigation, intervention or capital allocation may have the greatest potential value.
