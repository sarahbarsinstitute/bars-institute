Scope

BARS outputs are decision-support evidence, not a registered valuation, accounting opinion, or credit approval recommendation. They do not determine market value, loan terms, impairment recognition, or financial statement treatment. This calculator is a simplified, illustrative version of the underlying formulas set out in MET-BARS-001 v1.6 and does not represent an assessment output for any specific vineyard or block.

Virus impairment uses the logistic spread trajectory (β₀·Vₜ) and exponential yield-loss function (α) described in §4.1–4.2. Trunk disease impairment uses the vine-position progression model (κ) and replant-threshold (Aₘₐₓ) logic in §4.3, valuing affected-but-not-yet-replaced positions at full lost gross revenue (a disclosed conservative simplification, not a partial-productivity discount). Gap-rate fixed-cost dilution follows §4.3.1: full per-hectare operating cost charged against the physically missing/dead fraction, without a harvest-labour offset. Full BARS Assessments report P50/P95 outputs from a 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulation with correlated α/β₀ draws and PERT-drawn OPEX; this tool shows one central-estimate path with a fixed OPEX input only.

The BARS GTD Score band in this tool is quartered against Aₘₐₓ for the selected variety tier (25% increments, with Severe beginning at 75% of Aₘₐₓ), rather than a fixed universal D₀ scale. This anchors the score to the same replant threshold the progression model itself uses, but it is an internal working construction — no NZ research currently links D₀ to a severity threshold, and this quartering is not independently evidenced.
